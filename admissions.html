FAQs

Questions parents often ask

Which classes do you offer?

We have Nursery classes and Preparatory (Primary) classes. Nursery is a gentle, play-based start to school life. Preparatory builds strong foundations in literacy, numeracy and general knowledge, preparing pupils for the NPSE.

What are the school hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM. The school is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

How big are the classes?

We keep our classes small, so every child gets individual attention and close monitoring of their learning and behaviour.

Where can I buy the uniform?

All uniform items are available to buy from the school office. Girls wear a blue blouse and grey skirt; boys wear a blue shirt and grey trousers; everyone wears black shoes and grey socks.

Is Gaddiel a Christian school?

Yes. We are a Christian nursery and primary school. Our year includes a Carol Service and a Thanksgiving Service, and we are guided by Proverbs 22:6: “Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.”

Will my child be ready for the NPSE?

Our Preparatory curriculum prepares pupils for the National Primary School Examination (NPSE). Our first group of pupils will sit the NPSE next academic year, and our teachers are working hard to make sure they are well prepared and confident.
