How to install

Android phones

  1. Tap the yellow Download for Android button above.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open (or open your Downloads and tap Gaddiel-School.apk).
  3. If your phone asks, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source (Install unknown apps), then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. The Gaddiel icon is now on your home screen.

Android shows a warning for apps that do not come from the Play Store. This app comes from the school’s own website, www.gaddielschool.com.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open www.gaddielschool.com/app.html in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Using Opera Mini, a data-saver browser or Facebook’s built-in browser? Open the page in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) first.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and has no adverts.

Will it use a lot of data or space?

No. The Android download is about 2.6 MB, and pages you have already opened load without data.

Why is it not in the Play Store?

You don’t need the Play Store. You can download the app straight from the school’s website. The app always shows the latest information from our website, so you will rarely need to download it again.

How do I remove it?

Press and hold the Gaddiel icon on your home screen, then choose Uninstall or Remove.
