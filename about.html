Who we are

A young school with a big heart

Gaddiel Nursery & Preparatory School opened on 13 September 2021 and is steadily building a strong reputation in our community. We are still a young school, and we see our size as a strength. With 98 pupils this year, our teachers can give each child the time and care that many larger schools cannot.

Our motto, “Ep All Pikin Dem Fo Betteh”, means we are here to help every child, not just a few, grow in knowledge, confidence and good character.

What makes us different

Small class sizes

Teachers give each pupil more individual attention.

Close monitoring

We follow each child’s academic progress and behaviour closely and keep parents informed.

A family atmosphere

Our school is friendly, safe and welcoming to children and parents alike.

Our story

From 3 pupils to 98

The journey of Gaddiel Nursery and Preparatory School (GNAPS) began on 13 September 2021 with just three pupils, one teacher, a helper and a maintenance worker. God has been faithful. Our enrolment has grown every year since.

  1. 13 September 2021We open our doors with 3 pupils, one teacher, a helper and a maintenance worker.
  2. 2022/20239 pupils.
  3. 2023/202425 pupils.
  4. 2024/202557 pupils.
  5. 2025/202672 pupils.
  6. 13 September 2026We celebrate our 5th anniversary, giving thanks for five years of God’s grace and faithfulness.
  7. 2026/202798 pupils, the Director, six teachers, a helper and a maintenance worker.
  8. Next academic yearOur first group of pupils sits the National Primary School Examination (NPSE).

Thank you, Ms. Rachael Cole

We are deeply grateful to our Administrator/Teacher, Ms. Rachael Cole. Her hard work, commitment and single-handed effort in the early days laid the foundation for this success. All praise and thanks to her.

From a team of three, we have grown to a dedicated staff of the Director, six teachers, a helper and a maintenance worker. We trust God for more growth in the next academic year.

Our staff

A committed team

Our team has grown from three people to the Director, six teachers, a helper and a maintenance worker, including our Administrator/Teacher, Ms. Rachael Cole. Our teaching staff are dedicated to helping every pupil improve and succeed. They prepare lesson notes and schemes of work, follow the school curriculum carefully and work closely with parents.

  • A committed team focused on raising pupil performance
  • A growing curriculum covering both nursery and preparatory levels
  • Ongoing professional growth and quality teaching

Looking forward

Our first NPSE candidates

Gaddiel is well placed to grow. With small classes, committed staff and a strong focus on every learner, we offer an environment where children thrive. We remain focused on raising academic standards and expanding our impact in the community.

Next academic year marks an important milestone: our first group of pupils will sit the National Primary School Examination (NPSE). We are working hard to make sure they are well prepared and confident.
