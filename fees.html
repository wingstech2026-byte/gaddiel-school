How to pay

See the fees for each class in the fees list (PDF), or ask at the school office.

  1. Choose how to pay

    Orange Money, Afrimoney, bank transfer, or cash at the school office.

  2. Send the fee to the school

    Use only the numbers and accounts listed below. Write your child’s full name and class as the payment reference.

  3. Keep your receipt

    Keep the confirmation SMS, bank slip or office receipt.

  4. Tell us you’ve paid

    Fill in the short form below so the school office can match your payment quickly.

Payment details

For your safety: the school will never ask you to pay into a personal number that is not listed on this page. If someone asks you to, please call the school first.
