Our story

From 3 pupils to 98

The journey of Gaddiel Nursery and Preparatory School (GNAPS) began on 13 September 2021 in Freetown, Sierra Leone, with just three pupils, one teacher, a helper and a maintenance worker.

God has been faithful. Our enrolment has grown year after year: from 9 pupils in 2022/2023, to 25 in 2023/2024, 57 in 2024/2025, 72 in 2025/2026, and now 98 pupils in this academic year, 2026/2027. On 13 September 2026 we celebrated our fifth anniversary, giving thanks for five years of God’s grace and faithfulness.

We are deeply grateful to our Administrator/Teacher, Ms. Rachael Cole, whose hard work, commitment and single-handed effort at the start laid the foundation for this success.

From a team of three, we have grown to a dedicated staff of the Director, six teachers, a helper and a maintenance worker. Our motto, “Ep All Pikin Dem Fo Betteh”, means we are here to help every child do better. Small classes let our teachers give each child real individual attention.

Next academic year, our first group of pupils will sit the National Primary School Examination (NPSE), a proud milestone for our young school.
