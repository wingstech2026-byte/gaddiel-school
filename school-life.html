School life through the year

These events bring pupils, parents and staff together as one Gaddiel family.

First term

Carol Service and Christmas Party

We end the term with our Carol Service and a joyful Christmas Party.

Second term

Thanksgiving Service and Family Day

We go to church together for our Thanksgiving Service to give thanks to God, followed by a parade with a marching band. We also enjoy our Family Day field trip, when pupils and parents head out to Family Fun Outdoors for games and activities.

Third term

Nursery Graduation

We celebrate our youngest learners at the Nursery Graduation Ceremony, followed by a reception for pupils and their families.
